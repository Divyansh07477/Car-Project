Your Questions Answered

How much does it cost to rent a car

Rental prices very based on car type duration and season. Our rates start from $49/day for compact cars and go up to $299/day for luxary vehicles. Weekly and montly discounts are avaiable

What do I need to rent a car

You need a valid driver license a credit card in your name and proof of insurance International renters may need an International Driving Permit along with their license

Can I rent a car for someone else ?

The per renting the car must be present of pickup with thier licens and credit card; however,you can add additional drivers to the rental agreement for a small fee.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up up to 48 hours before pickup. Cancellation within 48 hours may insure a fee. No-shows are charged the full rental amount;

Is Insurence included in the rental?

Basic insurence is included in the rentals; Additional coverage options like collison damages and theft protection are available of checkout;

Can I return the car to a diffrent location?

Yes, one-way rental are available between most location; A one way fee may apply depending on the distence between pickup and drop out locations;
